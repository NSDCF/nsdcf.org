This scholarship is meant to honor the legacy of Randy Rovesti, an exceptional educator, coach, administrator, and community leader whose influence shaped generations of students and athletes in the Norwin School District.

Applicants must demonstrate academic commitment, athletic excellence, leadership, service to school/community and strong character.
