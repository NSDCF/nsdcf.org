requirements = [
  "Athletic"

Colin was a student at Norwin High School when a tragic accident took his life.  Colin was a joy to his family and friends, with great capacity to encourage and lift others around him.

Applicants must have participated in one or more NHS sports, provide evidence of school/community service, be known to give his/her best effort at all times and demonstrate the characteristics by which Colin lived.
